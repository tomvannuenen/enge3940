A map of our sentences

Everyone writes one sentence with the same word. A language model turns each sentence into numbers and places it on a map. Sentences it treats as similar land close together.

1. Write one sentence

Use the word cold. Any meaning is fine: weather, illness, a person, a drink. Write in English. Keep to everyday topics, and do not include names or personal details.

2. Find your sentence

Each dot is one sentence from the class. The red dot is yours. Which dots are near you? Look at the class screen and guess what they have in common.

Recap

  • The model places a sentence by the words around “cold”, not by a definition of the word.
  • Sentences about weather, illness and feelings often form separate groups.
  • The map shows which sentences are similar. It does not say why. For that you have to read them.

Which sentences ended up in a group you did not expect?

With a room code, your sentence goes to the class screen without your name and is deleted within 12 hours. The language model runs on the course's own service.
