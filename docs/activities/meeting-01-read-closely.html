Read a response closely

Close reading means looking at the exact words of a response and asking what they do. On this page you read three chatbot responses to the same riddle.

1. The question

A well-known riddle

What five-letter word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

The usual answer: “short”. Add “er” and it becomes “shorter”.

We changed one word and gave the riddle to several chatbots.

Our version

What five-letter word becomes longer when you add two letters to it?

2. First reading: what does it say?

Response 1 · Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite · saved 12 November 2026

The word is short. When you add two letters (-er) to it, it becomes shorter.

Which question does this response answer?

Look at the last word of the response. We asked for a word that becomes longer. The response says “it becomes shorter”. Yes. We changed one word in the question. Nothing changed in the answer. Not quite. It is the exact answer to the well-known riddle: “short” becomes “shorter”.

We ran our version 14 times on seven chatbots. 12 of the 14 responses said “short”.

3. Second reading: where do you look?

Another chatbot also said “short”, but it noticed the changed word.

Response 2 · Kimi K3 · saved 12 November 2026

Short — add “er” to it and you get “shorter,” which is literally a longer word (seven letters instead of five), even though it means the opposite!

That’s the twist on the classic riddle, which usually asks what word becomes shorter when you add two letters — same answer either way.

5. Your turn: a third response

A third chatbot got the same changed riddle.

Response 3 · Gemini 3.8 Flash · saved 12 November 2026

Technically, any five-letter word! Adding two letters makes it seven letters long, which is longer than it was before. (Note: You might be thinking of the classic trick riddle: “What five-letter word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?” The answer to that one is “Short” (add “er” to make it “shorter”).)

Which answer does this response give first?

Look at the first sentence. “Any five-letter word” answers our version. “Short” comes later, in the note. Yes. It answers our version first and then adds the well-known riddle, which nobody asked about.
