Agree or rewrite

Two readers often judge the same response differently. In this game your pair’s score is how often the two of you agree. The only way to raise it is to write a clearer criterion together.

Round 1

Do not talk yet. Someone asked a chatbot:

Request

Judge each response with this criterion: “The message is good.”

Now talk

Look at the responses marked “you disagreed”. Why did you judge them differently? What did each of you mean by “good”?

Write a clearer criterion together. Say who the message is for and what it should do. Either of you can type; you both see it.

Round 2

No talking again. Six new responses to the same request. Judge each with your own criterion:

Your criterion

Result

  • “Good” means different things to different readers. A criterion has to say what to look for.
  • If you still disagreed in round 2, look at where. A disagreement that survives a clear criterion is worth reporting.
  • For your poster: choose your criteria before you collect responses, and have two people judge.

What did your criterion say that “good” did not?

Your answers go to the class screen as a score for the pair, without names. Everything is deleted within 12 hours.
