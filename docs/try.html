Try an activity

This page shows an activity the way a class sees it, on one screen: the tutor’s screen on the left and four students on the right. Each student is a separate laptop.

  1. Choose an activity and press Start.
  2. Answer as each student. For pair activities, press “Start a pair” as Student 1 and type the pair number as Student 2.
  3. “Add 30 pretend students” fills the tutor’s screen with made-up answers, so you can see it with a full class.

The room and its answers are deleted within 12 hours, or when you close the room on the tutor’s screen.
